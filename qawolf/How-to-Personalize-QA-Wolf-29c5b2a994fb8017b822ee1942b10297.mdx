<Accordion title="How to: Log into QA Wolf">

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to [app.qawolf.com](http://app.qawolf.com/) and enter your email. You will receive a six-digit login code.

<Danger>
If you don't receive the email within five minutes, contact your team's QA Wolf representative to [request access to your workspace.](/qawolf/How-to-Personalize-QA-Wolf-29c5b2a994fb8017b822ee1942b10297)
</Danger>

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-5.png" alt="Screenshot of the QA Wolf login page" />
</Frame>

</Step>
<Step>
Enter the code. You will be taken to the Flows **tab**.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-6.png" alt="Screenshot of the QA Wolf login page" />
</Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How to: Set and change your user's avatar">

<Tip>
Your avatar appears next to your name in Activity records.
</Tip>

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the icon in the upper right to open the profile menu.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-7.png" alt="Screenshot of the QA Wolf login page" />
</Frame>
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Account Settings**
</Step>
<Step>
Go to **Profile**
</Step>
<Step>
If you have no image set, select or drag a file, then click **Upload**.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-8.png" alt="Screenshot of the QA Wolf login page" />
</Frame>
<br/>
<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-9.png" alt="Screenshot of the QA Wolf login page" />
</Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How to: Set a default workspace">
<Danger>
This is only applicable if you have been invited to more than one workspace.
</Danger>
<Steps>
<Step>
Click the icon in the upper right to view your **Account Settings**, which holds your name, email, avatar, and, if application, your default workspace setting.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-10.png" alt="Screenshot of the QA Wolf login page" />
</Frame>
</Step>
<Step>
Choose a new default from the **Default workspace** dropdown.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-11.png" alt="Screenshot of the QA Wolf login page" />
</Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>
</Accordion>
