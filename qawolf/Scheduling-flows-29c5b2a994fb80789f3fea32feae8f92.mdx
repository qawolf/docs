## Create a schedule

<Steps>
  <Step>
    In the **Runs** tab, choose the environment for your scheduled run from the list on the left.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Scheduled**, then **Schedule a run** on the right.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/execution-scheduling-bugs-and-maintenance/image-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    A drawer opens where you can configure your schedule:

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/execution-scheduling-bugs-and-maintenance/image-4.png)
    </Frame>

    a. **Name** lets you give your schedule a descriptive name.

    b. Set the Type to **Scheduled**.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/execution-scheduling-bugs-and-maintenance/image-5.png)
    </Frame>

    <Note>
      The other three options require some setup from QA Wolf. These are:

      - _Deployment_ – Triggers a run from a version control system (VCS) deployment hook.
      - _Pull Request Testing Button_ – Triggers a run from a custom button configured in your VCS.
      - _GitHub Deployment_ – Available for teams using GitHub-based deployments specifically.
    </Note>

    c. **Flows to run** lets you run _All flows_ or _Flows with selected tags_.

    d. **Concurrency limit** lets you set a maximum number of concurrent flows to control load on your system.

    <Danger>
      By setting the concurrency limit here, you will override the value in **`Workspace name → Workspace settings → Environment`**
    </Danger>

    e. **Frequency** lets you choose to schedule runs _Every day_ or _Every hour_.

    <Note>
      Once you’ve set up schedules for an environment, runs will appear in the Scheduled tab as soon as they are started.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing schedules

#### See all the schedules you have created

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open the **Runs** tab and click **Scheduled** from the lower left.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/execution-scheduling-bugs-and-maintenance/image-9.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

#### Pause a schedule

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Click the icon to the right of the schedule, then click **Pause**. Paused jobs will appear in grey text with a status of **Paused**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

#### Edit a schedule

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Click the icon to the right of the schedule, then click **Edit**. A drawer opens where you can edit your schedule.
  </Step>
</Steps>
