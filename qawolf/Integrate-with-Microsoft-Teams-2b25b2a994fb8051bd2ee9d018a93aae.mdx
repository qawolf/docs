### How to enable shared channels in Teams

<Note>
  Shared channels are **enabled by default** in Teams.
</Note>

QA Wolf provides a shared channel for your Teams integration. To use it, your organization must have shared channels enabled.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    In the [Microsoft Teams admin center](https://admin.teams.microsoft.com/), expand **Teams** in the left navigation bar and choose **Teams policies**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click the policy you want to confirm or change.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Under _Shared channels settings_, turn on **Join external shared channels** to allow collaboration with other orgs.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Apply** to save your changes.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/coverage-as-a-service/image-4.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How to enable external access

You must enable external access so QA Wolf team members can join your **Teams meetings** and your team can see QA Wolf's presence status (online/offline/away) in shared channels.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    In the [Teams admin center](https://admin.teams.microsoft.com/), expand **Users** in the left navigation bar and choose **External access**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Choose the appropriate option for allowing **Teams and Skype for Business users in external organizations**.

    - Do not select **Block all external domains**— this setting prevents the integration from working.
    - Allowing **qawolf.com** under **Allow only specific external domains** will ensure QA Wolf team members can participate while all other external domains remain blocked.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/coverage-as-a-service/image-5.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

### How to enable cross-tenant access in Microsoft Entra

To use a shared channel with QA Wolf, you must allow our Microsoft Entra tenant by adding QA Wolf as an allowed organization using our Microsoft Entra tenant ID.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    As a **Security Administrator**, sign in to the [Microsoft Entra admin center](https://entra.microsoft.com/).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Choose **External Identities** from the left navigation bar, then choose **Cross-tenant access settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    On the **Organizational settings** tab, click **Add organization**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    In the **Add Organization** drawer on the right, type QA Wolf's tenant ID: `3d41cc11-5772-457c-aad2-16b3924b22d0` and click Add at the bottom.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame>
  ![](/images/coverage-as-a-service/image-6.png)
</Frame>

If your default access settings restrict access, update your inbound and outbound B2B direct connect settings to allow QA Wolf.

Still under Organizational Settings, you should see QA Wolf listed under `n` **organizations found**.

<Frame>
  ![](/images/coverage-as-a-service/image-7.png)
</Frame>

#### To check access:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Click on the Configured link underneath Inbound access or Outbound access.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click the B2B direct connect tab.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Select the Customize settings radio button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On the **External users and groups** tab:

- For **Access status**, select _Allow access_
- For **Applies to**, select _All QA Wolf users and groups_

After updating these settings, verify that your default access configuration no longer blocks access to or from QA Wolf.

<Danger>
  Shared channel collaboration will not work if the default settings continue to block external tenants.
</Danger>

<Frame>
  ![](/images/coverage-as-a-service/image-8.png)
</Frame>

<Tip>
  We use shared channels so customers can access them without switching tenants, and B2B Direct must invite or join them. **B2B Collaboration** will not work (see [_MS doc_](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/solutions/collaborate-teams-direct-connect?view=o365-worldwide) for details).

  | B2B Collaboration               | B2B Direct Connect                        |
  | ------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
  | Applies to guest accounts       | Enables direct shared channel invitations |
  | Requires tenant switching       | Seamless access from own tenant           |
  | Only supports standard channels | Fully supports shared channels            |
</Tip>

We need your **Microsoft Entra tenant ID** and **domain name** so we can configure cross-tenant access on our side. Customers can locate their Tenant ID and domain on the [Microsoft Entra dashboard](https://entra.microsoft.com/#home).
