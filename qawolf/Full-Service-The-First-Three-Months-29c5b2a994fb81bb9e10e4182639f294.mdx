## title: "QA Wolf Full Service Onboarding" description: "What to expect during QA Wolf full service onboarding, including timelines, meetings, and setup steps." icon: "rocket" sidebarTitle: "Onboarding"

QA Wolf Full Service pairs your team with a dedicated QA engineering team that builds, runs, and maintains your automated test suite. This page walks through what onboarding looks like — from getting started to your first tests in production.

If you're evaluating QA Wolf, [book a demo](https://www.qawolf.com/demo) to talk through your coverage goals.

---

## Before onboarding begins

Onboarding typically kicks off 1 to 2 weeks after contract start, once a few prerequisites are in place.

### <Icon icon="message-circle" /> Communications

You'll connect a shared Slack, Teams, or Discord channel with the QA Wolf team. This is the primary channel for day-to-day communication throughout the engagement.

### <Icon icon="monitor" /> Test environment

You'll identify an environment for QA Wolf to run tests against. If you're not sure which to use, staging or a QA environment just below production is a good starting point.

- <Icon icon="clipboard-check" /> [Preparing an environment for QA Wolf test runs](/qawolf/Test-environments-2dc5b2a994fb800e8ce4fe36ee34764a)
- <Icon icon="layers" /> [Which environment should we run tests on?](/qawolf/Choose-the-right-CI-environment-to-execute-your-tests-29c5b2a994fb8031a3ddc1355eb4e7be)

Your team will send environment credentials to your QA Engineering Lead or Customer Success Manager via email, Slack, or a password manager like 1Password.

### <Icon icon="user-check" /> Champion

You'll identify a day-to-day point of contact — a Champion — who can clear blockers and make decisions about the test suite on your team's behalf.

## <Icon icon="flag" /> Kick-off meeting

**When:** Within 5 business days of contract start.

**Agenda**

- Introductions
- Review coverage goals
- Set expectations for the process
- Align on milestones

**Attendees**

| From your team | From our team |
| :-- | :-- |
| Point of contact from the sales process | Customer Success Manager |
| Champion (day-to-day contact) | QA Lead |
| Any other engineers or product managers you'd like to include |  |

**What to have ready**

- A connected Slack, Teams, or Discord channel
- Environment credentials

---

## <Icon icon="map-pin" /> Product tour meeting

**When:** Within 5 business days of contract start.

**Agenda**

- Walk through your app
- Identify testing priorities
- Identify areas to avoid

**Attendees**

| From your team | From our team |
| :-- | :-- |
| Product or engineering manager for each testable feature set | QA Engineering Lead |
|  | Test outliner |

**What to have ready**

- Internal alignment on which products QA Wolf will test and what areas are highest priority

---

## <Icon icon="clipboard-check" /> Test planning

Once the QA Wolf team understands your application and priorities, they'll begin outlining a Test Plan.

**What to send before the first review**

- Any existing test plans or priority lists — a spreadsheet, TestRail matrix, or a plain list all work
- Any existing E2E tests you want converted to QA Wolf format, or flagged as already-covered areas

### <Icon icon="clipboard-check" /> First Test Plan review

**When:** Within 15 business days of contract start.

**Agenda**

- Review the draft test plan
- Re-confirm priorities
- Provide feedback on coverage areas
- Confirm scope of work

**Attendees**

| From your team | From our team |
| :-- | :-- |
| Product or engineering manager for each testable feature set | QA Engineering Lead |
|  | Test outliner |

### <Icon icon="badge-check" /> Final Test Plan review

**When:** Within 20 business days of contract start.

**Agenda**

- Recap feedback from the first review
- Review updates to the test plan
- Review the AAA framework
- Finalize scope of work

**Attendees**

| From your team | From our team |
| :-- | :-- |
| Product or engineering manager for each testable feature set | QA Engineering Lead |
|  | Test outliner |

**What happens next**

- QA Wolf converts all approved outlines to the [AAA framework](https://www.qawolf.com/blog/intro-to-aaa) in preparation for coding
- Test creation begins and runs for the next 2 to 3 months

### <Icon icon="ban" /> Common test planning blockers

- Stakeholders aren't aligned on testing goals, which prevents finalizing the Test Plan
- Your team is unavailable to review the test plan or respond to product clarification questions

### <Icon icon="ban" /> Common test creation blockers

- The test environment is too unstable to run end-to-end tests reliably — see <Icon icon="clipboard-check" /> [Preparing an environment for QA Wolf test runs](/qawolf/Test-environments-2dc5b2a994fb800e8ce4fe36ee34764a)
