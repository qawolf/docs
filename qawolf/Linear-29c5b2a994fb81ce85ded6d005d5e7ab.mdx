---
title: "How to integrate with Linear"
description: "Connect QA Wolf to Linear to automatically create and sync bug report issues with your Linear team."
sidebarTitle: "Linear"
icon: ticket
---

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open the `Workspace name` dropdown and click **Workspace settings**.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/integrate-your-issue-tracker/image-7.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Navigate to **Integrations**.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/integrate-your-issue-tracker/image-8.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Enable** next to **Linear**. A modal opens.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Click **Connect**.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/integrate-your-issue-tracker/image-9.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Enter the **Team name** exactly as in Linear.

    <Danger>
      Team name is case sensitive.
    </Danger>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Enable the **Create bug report issue** toggle and optionally set **Label UUID** and **Assignee UUID** to set a default label and assignee for newly created issues.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/integrate-your-issue-tracker/image-10.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
