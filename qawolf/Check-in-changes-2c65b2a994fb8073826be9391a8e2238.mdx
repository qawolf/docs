sidebarTitle: "Check-in changes"

**Only changes to the current structure or behavior of your test suite require a review step:**

- Adding a new group
- Editing an existing group (moving flows in and out)
- Renaming a flow or group
- Editing an existing flow (code)

<Tip>
  New flows are automatically committed and published upon creation. They don’t modify any existing behavior, so no review is needed.
</Tip>

A <Icon icon="/images/image-1.png" /> green dot indicates that a flow or group has unpublished changes.
