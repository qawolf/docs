title: "Tailscale Client-based VPN"
description: "Connect QA Wolf to your private network through Tailscale by providing an access token or client configuration."
icon: "network"
sidebarTitle: "Tailscale Client-based VPN"
---

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Provide QA Wolf with either an access token or client configuration, depending on your VPN
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Confirm that your VPN mesh supports Linux clients and outbound connections from a dedicated proxy.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Ensure your network allows VPN traffic on TCP 443
  </Step>
</Steps>
