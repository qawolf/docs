⏱ **Time to complete:** 10–15 minutes

This guide walks you through the fastest way to get started with QA Wolf.

By the end of this guide you will have:

- connected your application
- mapped the workflows in your product
- generated your first automated flow
- executed a test run and reviewed the results

---

## Step 1: Connect your application

Start by connecting the version of your application you want QA Wolf to test.

An **environment** defines the app QA Wolf interacts with, along with configuration such as base URLs, users, and environment variables.

Once connected, QA Wolf can begin exploring your application.

Continue to: [Configure your workspace and environment →](/qawolf/Workspace-settings-2d25b2a994fb807fbd9bece4eb1a4d5f)

---

## Step 2: Map your application

After your environment is connected, QA Wolf can map the workflows in your application.

The **Map** provides a structured view of the user journeys that exist in your product. This helps you decide which paths should be automated first.

Continue to: [Map your application's workflows →](/qawolf/Map-your-app-s-workflows-2a45b2a994fb80ca8a87c8fdc2a443fc)

---

## Step 3: Generate your first flow

Choose a user journey and describe it in plain language.

QA Wolf generates the Playwright or Appium automation needed to validate that workflow. The resulting tests live in your **GitHub repository**, where your team can review and modify them like any other code.

Continue to: [Build and maintain flows using AI →](/qawolf/Build-and-maintain-flows-using-AI-2d35b2a994fb8084b524cfc9aca7c358)

---

## Step 4: Run your tests

Once your flow is created, run it to verify the workflow behaves as expected.

Runs execute tests across QA Wolf infrastructure and can be triggered:

- manually
- on a schedule
- from CI pipelines

Continue to: [Learn how runs work →](/qawolf/How-Runs-work-2a25b2a994fb8014b164d7ad2efd5dde)

---

## Step 5: Review results

Each run produces detailed results so you can understand what happened during execution.

Use the results to confirm your flow passed or to diagnose failures.

Continue to: [Interpret run results →](/qawolf/Interpret-run-results-2d55b2a994fb80199754e4b2d3ce0318)
