⏱ **Time to complete:** 10–15 minutes

<Tip>
  Prefer to work from Claude Code, Codex, or another coding agent? [Install the QA Wolf plugin](/qawolf/coding-agents/get-started) to explore your app and create your first flow from that client.
</Tip>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up to get started">
    Create your QA Wolf account — it's free to start. Already have one? [Sign in](https://app.qawolf.com/sign-in) instead.

    <Card title="Try for free" href="https://app.qawolf.com/sign-up" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Install CLI">
    Install and authenticate the QA Wolf CLI on your machine.

    Read more: [Authenticate the QA Wolf CLI →](/qawolf/local-execution/authenticate)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automate your first test">
    Outline your app's workflows, then ask the AI to generate a flow from one of them.

    Read more: [Create a coverage outline →](/qawolf/Map-your-app-s-workflows) and [Get the most out of AI flow creation →](/qawolf/getting-the-most-out-of-automate)
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If your application is behind a firewall or private network, a [VPN or network allowlist](/qawolf/QA-Wolf-s-static-IPv4) must be configured so QA Wolf infrastructure can reach it.
</Note>
