⏱ **Time to complete:** 10–15 minutes

## Before you begin

<Check>
  Before getting started, make sure the following are set up:

  - **You have access to a QA Wolf workspace.**\
    Your team’s QA Wolf administrator must invite you before you can log in and configure environments.
  - **QA Wolf can access the application you want to test.**\
    If your application is behind a firewall or private network, a **VPN or network allowlist** must be configured so QA Wolf infrastructure can reach it.
</Check>

## Step 1: Connect your application

Start by connecting to the version of your application you want QA Wolf to test.

An **environment** configures how QA Wolf accesses and interacts with your application, including base URLs, users, and environment variables.

Environments are typically configured once for each application environment, such as staging or production. and reused across all flows.

Once connected, QA Wolf can begin exploring your application.

Continue to: [Configure your workspace and environment →](/qawolf/Workspace-settings-2d25b2a994fb807fbd9bece4eb1a4d5f)

---

## Step 2: Map your application

After your environment is connected, QA Wolf can map the workflows in your application.

The **Map** provides a structured view of the user journeys that exist in your product. This helps you decide which paths to automate first.

Continue to: [Map your application's workflows →](/qawolf/Map-your-app-s-workflows-2a45b2a994fb80ca8a87c8fdc2a443fc)

---

## Step 3: Generate your first flow

Choose a user journey from the map or describe one in plain language.

QA Wolf generates the Playwright or Appium automation needed to validate that workflow (_Appium coming soon_). The resulting tests live in your **GitHub repository**, where your team can review and modify them like any other code.

Continue to: [Build and maintain flows using AI →](/qawolf/Build-and-maintain-flows-using-AI-2d35b2a994fb8084b524cfc9aca7c358)

---

## Step 4: Run your tests

Once your flow is created, run it to verify the workflow behaves as expected.

Runs execute tests across QA Wolf infrastructure and can be triggered:

- manually
- on a schedule

Continue to: [Learn how runs work →](/qawolf/How-Runs-work-2a25b2a994fb8014b164d7ad2efd5dde)

---

## Step 5: Review results

Each run produces detailed results so you can understand what happened during execution.

Use the results to confirm your flow passed or to diagnose failures.

Continue to: [Interpret run results →](/qawolf/Interpret-run-results-2d55b2a994fb80199754e4b2d3ce0318)
