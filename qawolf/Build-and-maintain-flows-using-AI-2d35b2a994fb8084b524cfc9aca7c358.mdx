## Create flows with the Automation AI

The easiest way to create tests is to use our Automation AI. Automation AI creates tests from plain language. Simply use the AI chat panel on the right side of the **Automate** Tab

<Frame>
  ![Screenshot 2026 03 26 At 2 51 20 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-03-26at2.51.20PM.png)
</Frame>

### Type a description of a flow

1. Type your instructions into the text box in the chat widget. Hit enter.

### Speak a description of a flow

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Click the <Icon icon="mic" /> icon and speak your instructions.

    <Note>
      Automation AI may prompt you to create the required groups and flows if they don’t already exist.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to import a third-party library with AI

<Steps>
  <Step>
    From the **Automate** tab, choose the flow you’ll be importing a library to.

    <Frame>
      ![Screenshot 2026 03 26 At 2 58 18 PM](/images/Screenshot2026-03-26at2.58.18PM.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Use the AI chat box in the right panel to request that the AI import a package for you—it will handle the entire process automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>
