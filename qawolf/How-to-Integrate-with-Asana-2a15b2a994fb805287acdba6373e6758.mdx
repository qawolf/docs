---
title: "How to: Integrate with Asana"
---
<Steps>
<Step>
Select **Workspace** settings from the `Workspace name` dropdown above the flows list.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/integrate-your-issue-tracker/image-11.png"/>
</Frame>
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Integrations** from the left-side navigation bar.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/integrate-your-issue-tracker/image-12.png"/>
</Frame>

</Step>
<Step>
Click **Enable** next to Asana.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Connect** on the modal

<Frame>
<img src="/images/integrate-your-issue-tracker/image-13.png"/>
</Frame>
</Step>
<Step>
Enable **Bug reports** and select the **Workspace** and **Project** in which bug reports are going to be created.

- Click **Disconnect** to disable the integration.
    <Frame>
    <img src="/images/integrate-your-issue-tracker/image-14.png"/>
    </Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>
