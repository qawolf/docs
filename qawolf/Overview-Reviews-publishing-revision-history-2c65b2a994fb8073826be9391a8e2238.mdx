---
title: "Overview: Reviews, publishing, and revision history"
description: "QA Wolf uses a Git-like change model for flows and groups: changes are reviewable, publish-controlled, and reversible. This ensures test updates are intentional and don’t affect scheduled runs until explicitly published."
---

**Only changes to the current structure of behavior of your test suite require a review step:**

- Adding a new group

- Editing an existing group (moving flows in and out)

- Renaming a flow or group

- Editing an existing flow (code)

<Tip>
New flows are automatically committed and published upon creation. They don’t modify any existing behavior, so no review is needed.
</Tip>
