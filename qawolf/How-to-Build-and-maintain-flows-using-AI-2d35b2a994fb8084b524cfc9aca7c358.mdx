## How to create flows and tests with the Automation AI

The easiest way to create tests is to use our Automation AI. Automation AI creates tests from plain language.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-61.png" alt="Screenshot of the Automation AI" />
</Frame>

### Type a description of a flow

1. Type your instructions into the text box in the chat widget. Hit enter.

### Speak a description of a flow

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the <Icon icon="microphone" /> icon and speak your instructions.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-62.png" alt="Screenshot of the Automation AI" />
</Frame>

<Note>
Automation AI may ask to create the required groups and flows if they don’t exist already.
</Note>
</Step>
</Steps>
## How to import a third-party library with AI

<Steps>
<Step>
From the **Flows** tab, choose the flow you’ll be importing a library to
</Step>
<Step>
Toggle **Code Mode** on. The right panel shows the **AI assistant** for that flow.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-63.png" alt="Screenshot of the Automation AI" />
</Frame>
</Step>
<Step>
Use the AI chat box in the right panel to request that the AI import a package for you—it will handle the entire process automatically.

<Frame>
<img src="/images/image-64.png" alt="Screenshot of the Automation AI" />
</Frame>
</Step>
</Steps>
