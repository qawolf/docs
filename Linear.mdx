## Requirements

- Access to a QA Wolf workspace
- A Linear team to create issues in

## How to connect QA Wolf to Linear

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open workspace settings">
    Open the **Workspace name** dropdown, click **Workspace settings**, then go to **Integrations**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Linear">
    Click **Enable** next to **Linear**, then click **Connect** in the modal.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/integrate-your-issue-tracker/image-9.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the integration">
    Enter the **Team name** exactly as it appears in Linear. The team name is case sensitive.

    Enable **Create bug report issue**. Optionally set **Label UUID** and **Assignee UUID** to give newly created issues a default label and assignee.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/integrate-your-issue-tracker/image-10.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
