QA Wolf's AI writes and maintains your flows. You set what it reaches, when it runs, and the conventions its code follows — all under **Workspace settings**.

These settings are what let QA Wolf run without you: flows that reach a private staging environment, start on their own when you deploy, and file what they find where your team already looks.
