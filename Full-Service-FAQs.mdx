### How can I see how many flows your team has built?

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open the Flows tab.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Select **All Flows** from the left sidebar. The total number of groups and flows appears on the right panel at the top.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/coverage-as-a-service/image-2.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

#### How can I see the number of active flows your team has built?

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Set your filter using the <Icon icon="filter" /> icon.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Hover over your team name in **All Flows** above to see the <Icon icon="square" /> icon, then click it to get the <Icon icon="square-check" /> icon. Below the list to the right, the number to the right of the <Icon icon="route" /> icon shows the total number of flows, and the number to the right of the <Icon icon="git-commit-vertical" /> icon shows the total number of tests that meet the filter criteria.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/coverage-as-a-service/image-3.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>
