## Requirements

- Access to a QA Wolf workspace
- An Asana workspace and project to create bug reports in

## How to connect QA Wolf to Asana

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Enable the Asana connection from **Workspace settings → Integrations**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the integration">
    Enable **Bug reports**, then select the **Workspace** and **Project** to create bug reports in.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To disable the integration, click **Disconnect**.
