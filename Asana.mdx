## Requirements

- Access to a QA Wolf workspace
- An Asana workspace and project to create bug reports in

## How to connect QA Wolf to Asana

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open workspace settings">
    Open the **Workspace name** dropdown above the flows list, click **Workspace settings**, then go to **Integrations**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect Asana">
    Click **Enable** next to **Asana**, then click **Connect** in the modal.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/integrate-your-issue-tracker/image-13.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the integration">
    Enable **Bug reports**, then select the **Workspace** and **Project** to create bug reports in.

    <Frame>
      ![](/images/integrate-your-issue-tracker/image-14.png)
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

To disable the integration, click **Disconnect**.
