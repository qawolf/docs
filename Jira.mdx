## Overview

- **Automatic issue handling.** Creates Jira issues automatically when new bug reports are generated in QA Wolf, keeping Jira up to date without manual entry. Context added in Jira is not written back to QA Wolf.
- **Synchronization.** Closing a bug report in QA Wolf closes the linked Jira ticket, and closing a Jira ticket updates QA Wolf. QA Wolf also performs regular bug revalidations to ensure accuracy. Contact your QA Wolf representative if a closed ticket doesn't update QA Wolf.
- **Test creation requests.** Add a **QA Wolf** label to a Jira ticket to automatically create a coverage request in QA Wolf.
- **Custom configuration.** Specify the project, assignee, and issue status. Currently, each integration setup supports one project, one assignee, and one status type.
- **Custom fields.** Add custom Jira fields to bug reports if your Jira project uses them. Custom fields apply globally across all tickets created through QA Wolf, not per flow.

<Note>
  **Full Service customers:** the QA Wolf team can configure this integration on your behalf. To get started, invite QA Wolf as a user in your Jira workspace using this email convention: qa\+\[yourcompanyname\]@qawolf.com (e.g. [qa\+acme@qawolf.com](mailto:qa+acme@qawolf.com)).
</Note>

## Requirements

- Access to a QA Wolf workspace
- A Jira project to create issues in

## How to connect QA Wolf to Jira

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    Enable the Jira connection from **Workspace settings → Integrations**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Authorize the connection">
    Select the Jira team to link to your QA Wolf workspace and accept.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Configure the integration">
    Choose your Jira **Project**, **Issue Type**, and **Assignee**, then enable **Create bug issue report** and **Close bug issue report**.

    <Tip>
      Ask your QA Wolf representative to configure any custom fields you need, such as the issue description.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>
