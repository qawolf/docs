Manage who is in your workspace under **Settings > Members**.

## Who can do what

- **Anyone in the workspace** — invites people by email. Their invitations use the **Member** role.
- **Membership managers** — see the members table, choose the role for an invite, change roles, remove members, and turn domain sign-up on or off.

People who are not membership managers see a notice in place of the members table.

## Invite people

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite dialog">
    Go to **Settings > Members** and click **Invite**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add email addresses">
    Type or paste one or more addresses. Separate them with commas, semicolons, spaces, or new lines. Each address becomes its own chip.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a role">
    Membership managers pick a role from **Role**. It defaults to **Member**, and you can only pick roles you are allowed to give.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Send invite**. Everyone on the list gets an invite email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

QA Wolf flags an address that is not valid, that already belongs to a member, or that already has a pending invite. Remove it before sending.

If an address is on an email domain your workspace does not use yet, QA Wolf asks you to confirm before sending the invite.

## Find and manage members

The members table lists everyone in the workspace and every open invitation, with each person's name, email, role, and when they were last active.

- **Search** — click the search icon and type part of a name or email.
- **Role** — filter the table to one or more roles. The menu shows how many people hold each role. Click **Reset role** to clear the filter.

Open the menu at the end of a row to act on it:

- **Copy email** — copies the person's email address. Available on every row.
- **Change role** — grants or revokes a role. A member always keeps at least one role.
- **Remove** — removes the member from the workspace.
- **Resend invitation** — sends a pending invite again.
- **Cancel invite** — withdraws a pending invite.
- **Send new invitation** — replaces an expired invite with a new one.

You cannot change your own membership from the table, and members managed by Directory Sync only offer **Copy email**.

## Let your email domain join without an invite

Domain sign-up lets anyone with your company's email address join the workspace on their own. They sign up, skip the invite, and join as members.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members">
    Go to **Settings > Members** as a membership manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on domain sign-up">
    Check **Anyone with a `<your domain>` email can join**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

To stop it, uncheck the same box. People who already joined stay in the workspace. Someone on your domain who signs up while it is off sees that your domain already has a workspace and is asked to get an invite from your team.

<Note>
  You need a verified email on the domain to turn domain sign-up on. When single sign-on or Directory Sync manages who joins the organization, nobody joins by email domain and the box is locked. See [Configure Single Sign-On (SSO)](/sso).
</Note>
